03 / THE ROAD AHEAD

Start with the gauge.
Grow with the build.

The platform is being designed to grow across vehicles. Development begins with stock Jeep JKs and Hemi-swapped Jeep JKs, then expands to other enthusiast platforms as interfaces and vehicle-specific behavior are validated.

Current priority, subject to engineering results and customer input:

  1. Initial targetStock Jeep JK and Hemi-swapped Jeep JK
  2. Adjacent platform workBroader Mopar vehicles, building on JK and Hemi overlap
  3. Evaluation vehiclesVolvo C30 and Porsche 718, which we can access for research and testing
  4. Lower priority candidatesLexus GX470 and Toyota FJ Cruiser
FIRST PRODUCT

Standard gauge size

A configurable, multi-page display designed for a conventional round gauge location, paired with an Android setup experience. Final dimensions, fitment, and compatibility are still being defined.

FUTURE DIRECTION

More ways to fit

Larger dials, OLED displays, and custom dashboard designs for vehicles that call for a different shape or installation.

FUTURE DIRECTION

Deeper vehicle data

A flexible data architecture for OBD-II and CAN interfaces, including multiple interfaces in swap applications. Direct CAN integration and each vehicle-specific data path will be validated separately.

EXPLORATORY

Advanced control and customization

Potential vehicle-specific controls include raising idle for onboard air or winching, and managing traction control or ABS behavior where supported. We also plan to study existing vehicle knowledge and use AI to help discover, publish, and prioritize a roadmap that prospective customers can influence. Every control will require platform-specific engineering and validation.

EXPLORATORY

Shared road awareness

Future integrations could bring opt-in, crowdsourced alerts for road hazards and police presence, plus a touch flow for reporting hazards when safe. Report accuracy, freshness, coverage, privacy, and safe presentation would need to be worked through before release.

When can I buy one?+

Preorders are being prepared for the standard-size round gauge. No price or date has been announced, and checkout is not open yet. This page will show the final offer and terms before orders begin.

How is my contact information used?+

We store your email and selected interests to contact you about eGauge preorder plans. Custom project details are used to review and respond to that request. We do not sell this information. To request removal, use the project request form and say you want your information removed.

How will deposits and VIP input work?+

We are exploring refundable deposits for the first units and a VIP path for people who want to help shape the roadmap. Amounts, benefits, refund terms, and limits will be published before either opens. Joining the interest list is free and does not reserve a unit.

Will it work with my vehicle?+

The long-term goal is a platform that can work across vehicles through appropriate OBD-II or CAN hardware. Development starts with stock Jeep JKs and Hemi-swapped Jeep JKs. Data access and control functions vary by vehicle, so we will publish tested compatibility and supported features for each platform before making those claims.

What is working today?+

The round display and touch controls have been tested on prototype hardware. An Android phone has configured the gauge and installed development firmware. Complete vehicle communication and driving behavior still need validation.

Are the future features part of the first gauge?+

No. Larger and OLED displays, custom dash designs, direct CAN integration, multiple interfaces, vehicle controls, crowdsourced road alerts, and AI-assisted customization are future directions. Stock and Hemi-swapped Jeep JK work is the starting platform; broader vehicle support will be built and validated in stages.

Is this the final design?+

No. The displays on this page are interactive concepts using sample readings. The enclosure, final feature set, and production specifications are still being developed.
