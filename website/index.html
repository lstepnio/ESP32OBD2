03 / THE ANDROID APP

Your setup.
One place.

Build a dashboard, see your car's fault information, and adjust the gauge. One connection indicator brings phone, gauge, and vehicle status together.

Alerts with context.

Set thresholds for any selectable reading, including transmission values. Threshold and check-engine events share severity, history, and context, with optional phone notifications.

One vehicle. Optional second adapter.

Most vehicles use one OBD-II adapter for available engine and transmission data. Some swaps, including our Hemi-swapped Jeep, have separate ECM and TCM diagnostic ports and need two adapters to read both at once. This is uncommon outside swaps. Expert associates the second adapter with the same vehicle; pages and alerts stay together.

Advanced fault filters.

Choose exact Engine or Transmission codes to hide from ordinary views. The gauge can suppress a controller's CEL indication only when fresh, complete checks account for every reported code. Raw diagnostic evidence is retained.

Android and the autonomous gauge are the current focus. Phone reading previews are for setup; live companion telemetry and iOS are future work.

04 / PROJECT PROGRESS

Working today.
Proving what is next.

A working development platform, with testing kept separate from promises. The current vehicle is a Hemi-swapped 2010 Jeep Wrangler with a JSS transmission setup.

OBSERVED ON HARDWARE

Engine and transmission, together

Live engine RPM has matched the Jeep's dash. Transmission temperature and gear display on the gauge. Simultaneous ECM and TCM readings, independent adapter loss and return, and operation without the phone have been checked on this setup.

OBSERVED ON HARDWARE

Setup and update recovery

Saved configuration and signed App/Wi-Fi firmware installation are working. Repeated Wi-Fi sessions and controlled interrupted-update checks have passed with saved setup retained. Longer loaded tests and physical power-loss cases remain.

NEXT IN QUALIFICATION

More time in the vehicle

Longer dual-adapter sessions, startup with a missing source, reading freshness, real fault and threshold alerts, live CEL filtering, and daylight and night legibility.

NEXT IN QUALIFICATION

A broader compatibility matrix

More adapters, vehicles, phones, and accessibility checks. Catalog coverage is not a compatibility list, and one successful Jeep setup does not establish support for every swap or stock vehicle.

Will it work with my vehicle?+

What is working today?+

The gauge displays live engine and transmission readings on the tested Jeep setup. The Android App configures pages, alerts, and display settings, and installs signed development firmware over private Wi-Fi. Independent adapter recovery and selected interrupted-update cases have been checked. Broader vehicle compatibility and sustained-load testing remain open.

Are the future features part of the first gauge?+

No. Optional dual OBD-II adapters are already implemented for the tested swap setup, with broader qualification still in progress. Larger or OLED displays, custom dash designs, direct CAN, sensor-driven Auto orientation, vehicle controls, and phone-delivered road alerts remain future work. No high-idle, ABS, or traction-control action is enabled today.

Is this the final design?+

No. The screen gallery reflects the current gauge and Android interface; the road-awareness section shows future possibilities. The enclosure, final fitment, production specifications, and supported-vehicle list are still being developed.
