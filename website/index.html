ORDERS AND CUSTOM WORK

Tell us what you’re building.

Ask about a gauge, or tell us about your own dashboard, display, control panel or integration. We’ll follow up on availability, scope and pricing.

Custom work can include LCD, OLED or e-paper displays, software for new or existing hardware, and CAN bus or accessory controls. We can also help capture and investigate signals remotely.

We use your details only to reply. Sending a request does not place an order.
