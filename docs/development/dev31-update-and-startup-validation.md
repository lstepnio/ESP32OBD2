# dev.31 update and transfer-start investigation

On September 29, 2026 the Pixel 10 Pro installed the signed `0.2.0-dev.31` prerelease through the app and gauge Wi-Fi path. The app reported that the gauge confirmed the new image running. The downloaded release catalog was independently checked against the pinned development public key at generation 21. The bundle SHA-256 was `9fc40e3dca441aba1d251d948711b35c5735fc2a4733849a7ccf529d568ef09d`; the contained 1,495,520-byte image was version `0.2.0-dev.31`, had the expected board tag, and its signed image SHA-256 was `38bfe9d5e68fbd3ccc7df3b66c61e09dfe4c3c1a8c7145f205ee8e3769b15f41`.

The Pixel Settings screen read back brightness 100%, rotation 90 degrees, Metric units, and automatic page cycling Off from the gauge after installation. The user observed `PAIR` on the physical gauge. A later attempt to set five-second cycling ended with an unconfirmed connection, so cycling remains Off and physical cycling behavior and color appearance are unverified. Pixel Bluetooth diagnostics showed repeated bond attempts and PIN requests. A subsequent serial connection caused a gauge reset and its boot log showed an owner pairing window; this confirms that the owner association was absent at that boot, but does not establish when or why it was cleared. Firmware supports a physical 12-second hold to forget the owner. No hold has been confirmed. Re-pairing and another authenticated read are required before further gauge writes.

Source review found a possible unintended reset path: the touch handler measured a 12-second owner-reset hold using a `pressed_at` timestamp initialized to zero, without requiring a matching press event. An orphaned release after 12 seconds of uptime could therefore invoke owner reset. The follow-up requires a matching press and logs both owner-load and physical-reset events. This is a preventive fix, not proof that the orphaned release caused the observed loss.

During the dev.31 update the app displayed `0% sent` for a long pre-transfer period, then advanced quickly to 100%. Source review found that `TRANSFERRING` and progress zero were published before Android joined the temporary Wi-Fi network, before the signed metadata was sent, and before `esp_ota_begin` erased flash. The firmware passed `OTA_SIZE_UNKNOWN`, which asks ESP-IDF to prepare the whole 3 MiB OTA slot despite the 1.5 MiB image. The follow-up passes the verified image length to `esp_ota_begin`, moves the transfer stage after flash preparation, and logs durations for BLE preflight, gauge access-point setup, Android network availability, TCP connection, and flash preparation. The next signed update must measure those timings and confirm the new image on the physical gauge before claiming a speed improvement.

The signed dev.32 prerelease was published from `efa6c02b97bb7c0504db4b02d78920c7213a60ad` with catalog generation 22. The independently downloaded catalog and image signatures verified against the pinned development public key. Bundle SHA-256: `d7e7eb59461d2db81812d7d520cbff032a3a25820f76250cb6046c02b54e7be2`. Image SHA-256: `00fa168a101fabd3314c4e2798a5a9e33db9f8eb181c0f98979c12da1f0528a4`. The image is 1,496,240 bytes and embeds `0.2.0-dev.32`. The PR's Android, firmware, and contract checks passed. Physical installation awaits restored owner pairing.
