Decision and rationale

One Needs attention list replaces overlapping Vehicle health and recent Alerts panels. Direct detail removes the generic controller/fault landing step. History, Alert settings and Vehicle actions remain direct utilities; Advanced owns hidden codes and the optional child adapter. Connection/retry status stays in the universal pill. The model remains one logical vehicle regardless of its physical adapters.

Hidden means hidden across App and gauge presentation. The sole UI exception is rule management in Advanced. Keep raw immutable evidence internally; hiding must be reversible and never clear vehicle codes or fabricate resolution. Saved vehicle intent governs App/offline visibility; each gauge retains its last acknowledged configuration. This deliberately replaces the previous live-confirmed-only App filter and raw-history/normal-export exceptions.

Current authoritative behavior lives in the [shared alert contract](../architecture/alerts-and-diagnostics.md#advanced-vehicle-scoped-cel-code-filters), [design system](design-system.md#current-component-and-messaging-rules) and [Android storage contract](../architecture/android-runtime.md#storage-and-recovery). This record preserves review context rather than duplicating operating instructions.

Audit that motivated the change

At review time, ui/car/CarScreen.kt and ui/AlertPresentation.kt exposed overlapping health/episode summaries.
